1. Deep Sea Diving - the grid while sailing
There are 13 different ocean floor areas. Each one is depicted in the
grid below by the first letter of the name of the ocean floor area and
cooresponds to the sea floor map below that.

Each letter in the grid is a 10x10 metron square of the ocean. Meaning,
if you dive and are in "Cave around an undergroun volcano" depicted by
the letter C, if you move your ship east 10 metrons, you will then dive
and be in "On an Abysmal floor" depicted by the letter O.

Raising the bell while any individual remains on the ocean floor kills
them.

Notes about deep sea diving are below the individual maps.

5. Deep Sea Diving - techniques, tips, and tricks
1.  Leadlines are your friend. Any shipfitter can make one for you.
      TRADEWHO SHIPFITTING. It requires 10 leather and 40 rope.
      SHIP TAKE SOUNDING will tell you ahead of time if it's a good
      location to dive and deep sea fish.
2.  If you do not have a diamond-tipped shovel, take extras. They will
      break.
3.  Post one person topside deep sea fishing to serve as look out.
4.  Do not raise the bell until everyone is accounted for.
5.  You can return topside as soon as you are in the bell, while it
      remains on the ocean floor just SHIP LEAVE BELL. You cannot
      re-enter the bell until it's topside.
6.  You do not have to raise the bell the entire way each time you dive.
      Once you see brightness you can SHIP LOWER BELL. "The bell
      continues its ascent into the brightness above."
7.  Recommendations: Kill the aggressives, they are more likely to drop
      a key. Kill the other creatures. Dig the entire area. Then if you
      still have not found a key and have a steel-encased strongchest,
      TURN CHEST HANDLE RIGHT.
8.  Most of your dives will be one bell. Anything above one bell is more
      likely to have a sea dragon. Those happen once a month, per sea
      floor area.
9.  When you cook, severed fingers are worth good gold, if one falls
      out of the sea creature keep it, AB SHIPFITTING SCRIMSHAW.
10. Pale bone chests - good gold, lots of risk. You open these up with
      the skeleton keys made through shipfitting. At the moment you
      unlock the chest, you have a random chance of summoning a sea
      monster. Beware, I did not summon a level 1, I summoned a level 5.
      2 chomps and my cutter was sunk. So get the gold and put it in
      your pack, FAST and take necessary steps for this potential event.
11. CLHELP DEEPSEATREASURE in the Hashan Navy clan gives you a list of
      the items you might dig up, when digging on the ocean floor.
12. All deep sea creatures can be used to cook; except dragons, spiders,
      hippocampi, and merrow.
13. You can butcher all creatures before cooking them.
14. Activating Helm Aura level 5 is 20 pts to crew morale per creature
      as you cook them.
15. Deckhand level 1 or 2 adds 200 common food to larder per creature.
      Deckhand level 3 to 5 adds 200 good food to larder per creature.
16. A Sailors' Decree artefact allows you to specialise anywhere on
      land.
      You do not need to sail to the specialists. ARTSEARCH SEAFARING
