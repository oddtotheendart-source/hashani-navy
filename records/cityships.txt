This scroll contains information of interest regarding the Hashan Navy,
Ministry of Maritime Affairs, and the vessels of the fleet.

=== RULES OF OPERATION ===

When taking out or returning any city ship, make sure you check the
following:

STRONGBOX GOLD:
Return this to the original amount when you leave the ship, unless
you're doing a city service such as a marque run, training run or
monster hunt for Navy profit.

TOKENS:
These can be viewed using SHIP STORES CHECK.
Each ship should have a total of three tokens per denizen crew member.
For the cutters this is 126, for striders 318 and for the galleys 393.
If you take a ship out and the number is below this, notify the
Minister, or if you're an aide, withdraw funds and fix it.
If it's below two per crew member (84 for the Cutters, 212 for the
Striders and 262 for the Galley) then do not take that ship out until it
is fixed.
If you sink a ship, token stores need to be returned to three per member
after salvaging. For more info on salvaging, see the section below.

MANOEUVRES:
Every ship should be left on EVASIVES, and if you change these during
your trip, you should ensure that they are changed back once you finish
your trip.

FLAGS:
All ships should be flying a Hashani emblazoned flag.
Pre-emblazoned flags can be purchased at cost from Small Wicked Things
in Hashan.

GEAR:
It is recommended to have a ship's axe for each crew member on board,
along with sufficient buckets and rope for dousing fires.
In case of emergency, tokens for adventurer crew can be purchased from
Captain Maelstrom; these will return the user to Tasur'ke port when
used, though they will deplete a very large chunk of your endurance and
willpower reserves.
**Note Maelstrom sells two types of tokens, the etched ones for 5,000
gold and the blank ones for 100 gold. Anyone with near trans seafaring
can etch the blank ones, and this is a much cheaper way of procuring
them.

COMMSCREEN:
Keep this lowered at all times on city ships, to allow contact with the
mainland to be unhindered.

BOARDING/CREW PERMS:
It is illegal to bring non-Hashani aboard Hashani naval ships for any
reason, for more information read CHELP NAVALPOLICY.

PIRACY:
Hashani naval ships are only to be used to attack enemies of the Court
or others with good reason, for more information read CHELP NAVALPOLICY.


== REGARDING SINKING AND SALVAGING ==

If you get a city ship sunk while on Navy business and obeying the rules
for borrowing ships, the Navy will cover the costs. This includes things
like marque runs for citizens, training runs, events for the city or
endeavours such as fishing trips or monster hunts where the profit is
going towards the Navy directly.

If you are using a ship for personal use or a Trade deal, you must cover
the costs of salvage including replacing tokens and food stores.

To salvage a ship travel to the nearest harbour (any harbour, manmade or
not) and HARBOUR SALVAGE SHORTNAME. Once it has been salvaged at the
following midnight, you must re-equip it with token and food stores, and
order the crew to repair it. If you are confused or in any way unsure
about how to salvage a ship and re-equip it, DO NOT PROCEED. Message the
Minister or an aide, make a log note to the Navy Clan and be prepared to
refund costs when the ship is salvaged.

While you can salvage a ship with another ship, this is not usually
considered efficient, and in many cases can be highly dangerous and time
consuming so it is not recommended. Only do so if you are experienced.

**TOKENS SHOULD BE RETURNED TO THE LEVEL OF 3 PER DENIZEN CREW NUMBER
AFTER SINKING. THIS IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT, AND FORGETTING TO DO SO WILL
RISK YOU LOSING YOUR CAPTAIN PERMISSIONS.**


=== PIRACY ===

If you are sailing a city ship or a ship owned by a citizen flying a
Hashani or Hashani Navy flag and you are attacked without provocation,
log the encounter and result along with anyone taking part on both
sides, to the Hashani Navy clan. This is a crime, and they can be
charged accordingly.


=== WINDCUTTERS ===

HNS Black Wave** (HNBW), located in Orilla
HNS Silver Spectre** (HNSS), located in the Ilyrean Isles
HNS Vasselart (HNVA), located in Shastaan

These ships are not to be moved from their general areas (Ilyrean Isles,
Meropis) unless given permission. HNBW must be returned to Orilla once
your trip is over (do NOT strand it at Prin, unless given permission!)

STRONGBOX: Should remain between 10,000 and 20,000 gold.
TOKENS: 126
STORES: Should remain over 10,000 stores of any type.
AMMO: Should have at least 50 flares of any colour.


=== SEASTRIDERS ===

HNS Shadow Tempest (HNST)
HNS Edge of Night (HNEN)

These ships must be returned to any mainland harbour after use.

STRONGBOX: Should remain between 10,000 and 25,000 gold.
TOKENS: 318
STORES: Should remain over 5000 stores of any type.
AMMO: Should have at least 1000 of each ammo, and 50 flares of any
colour.


=== GALLEYS ===

HNS Grey Wind (HNGW)
HNS Verdict of the Court (HNVC)

These ships must be returned to any mainland harbour after use.

STRONGBOX: Should remain between 15,000 and 30,000 gold.
TOKENS: 393
STORES: Should remain over 5000 stores of any type.
AMMO: Should have at least 1000 of each ammo, and 50 flares of any
colour.
