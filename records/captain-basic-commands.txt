Composed by: Rhydian on or about the 8th of Glacian, 883 AF.

This is a quick rundown of some of the basic ship commands you will
likely be using often as a captain. Thank you to Ilsefi for composing
this list!

Ship Abilities:
      - INVOKE SEA SPIRIT
*Must be done at the figurehead, this is to meld so you can use
seaspells.
      - SEASPELL INVESTED - See seaspells prepared for your journey
      - AURA STATUS - See auras that are up
      - SHIPWARNING ON - See what ships are moving around you.

Ammunitions:
      - SHIP AMMO
      - SHIP AMMO STATUS
      - SHIP AMMO ADD <crate#>
      - SHIP AMMO REMOVE <#> <ammunitiontype>

Loading weapons:
      - LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo>
      - LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo> DIPPED

Unloading weapons:
      - UNLOAD <ammo> from <weapon>

Firing weapons:
      - FIRE <weapon> AT <seamonster>
      - FIRE <weapon> AT <shiptarget>

If the ship has locked on to a target:
      - FIRE <weapon>
Ship targeting:
      - SHIP TARGET LOCK <ship>
      - SHIP TARGET LOCK CLEAR

Clearing entanglements:
      - CHOP TETHER when hookshots have grappled.
      - CLIMB RIGGING , CLEAR RIGGING
      - CLEAR FIGUREHEAD
      - CLEAR <weapon>
*These abilities are channeled so you cannot move until the execution is
complete.

Fire control:
       -  FILL BUCKET WITH WATER (requires rope and empty bucket)
       -  DOUSE ROOM|GROUND
       -  DOUSE <person>|<item>
       -  DOUSE FIRE - Be in crows nest and this'll douse sails
*Must have a bucket and rope in hand to execute these actions upon a
ship.

Spyglass:
       - SPYGLASS
       - SPYGLASS LOOK
*This will display any seamonsters or ships in a small radius around
your ship.
       - SPYGLASS INFO <seamonster>
*This will show you specific information about a seamonster.
