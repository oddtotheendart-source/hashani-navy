TRADE REWARDS

A breakdown of the rewards available through ship trading (CLHELP
SHIPTRADES).


Morale/Experience:
Increases by a set amount. Morale is about 400 points, experience does
not seem to scale to current crew levels. Not worthwhile except for a
newling crew up to Apprentice rank.

Gold:
65,000, 75,000, 100,000, or 200,000

Bound Credits:
5, 10, or 25

Weapon Ammunition:
Ammo for ship weapons.

A Barrel of Rum:
You can DRINK out of it. Self-refilling, lasts 50 Achaean Months.

A Striking Sea Captain's Hat:
Tricorn hat made out of ostrich skin. PUSH or PULL to make your crew
salute you. Lasts 200 months.

A Great Notic Amulet:
Amulet crafted in worship of the Caspeid Notia. Attach to figurehead and
turn amulet to petition for a blessing. WARNING: The Caspeid's
capricious nature may induce her to curse you rather than bless you.
Tinker at your own risk.

A Hollowed Skull:
Use it to play the game Safe Passage. READ SKULL for instructions.

An Unmarked Crate:
Random ship furniture. Includes items like: Rope, lantern, iron bitts,
sloop chest, etc. Purely decorative.

A Pair of Seaworn Dice:
Historically worthless for the price of the trade. Throw dice for a
random reward.

A Black-finned Flare:
Light the flare to reveal all the ships in the same sea or ocean as your
vessel. Must be on the water to work.

A Birdcage Covered in White Cloth:
Randomly contains (in order of rarity) a budgie/cockatiel or parrot. Can
be taught a single word by practicing repeatedly.

A Golden Gryphon:
Legendary, flying mount.

A Reticulated Giraffe:
Legendary mount.

A Wicker Basket Containing a Ginger Ship Kitten:
A kitten to live on your ship. It will wander from room to room, and
gradually grow older.
