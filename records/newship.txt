clhelp newship
Composed by: Kuriel on or about the 16th of Glacian, 829 AF.

Congratulations on your new ship! This checklist will help ensure that
you get the new vessel fully outfitted and ready for the rigors of the
sea, at minimal cost and with only slight inconvenience. As ever, make
sure to read the various clan scrolls and to ask for help when
necessary. We also strongly recommend new captains pursue at least Petty
Officer status in the Navy, via CLHELP PROGRESSION, to fill out any gaps
in their sailing knowledge.

CREW

1) See CLHELP CREW, for relevant numbers.
2) Visit Thraasi, Tasur'ke, or Shastaan and HARBOUR CREW FORHIRE to see
how many are available to hire. Use HARBOUR commands to hire. We
recommend hiring the maximum crew on all vessels.
3) You can hire crew to any ship, from those harbours. So even if your
ship is docked in Tasu'rke, you can hire crew from Shastaan. Presumably
they walk to your ship on their own.
4) Check SHIP CREW from anywhere on the ship, to confirm status.

STORES

1) The basic harbour commands are HARBOUR PURCHASE # TOKEN STORES FOR
shipname and HARBOUR PURCHASE # COMMON/GOOD/FINE STORES FOR shipname.
2) We recommend purchasing token stores equal to your total number of
crew, times three. We recommend purchasing 10 common stores initially,
to get your crew eating.
3) If you have access to bait fish, cooking is often a better option for
filling your stores, because it increases crew morale (especially in
conjunction with the helm aura) while also filling your larders. But the
specifics of cooking and increasing morale are beyond the scope of this
scroll.
4) Use the guidelines in HELP CREW to set rations. Better rations
increase morale faster.
5) Check SHIP STORES from the quarterdeck or cabin, to confirm status.

STRONGBOX

1) We recommend placing no more than 20-30k gold in your strongbox, to
prevent very large plunders from enemy ships cleaning you out entirely.
2) In the cabin, SHIP STRONGBOX DEPOSIT # gold.
3) Use the guidelines in HELP CREW to set wages. Better wages increase
morale faster.
4) SHIP STRONGBOX CHECK in the cabin, to confirm status

AMMO AND WEAPONS

1) Ask the Navy clan for low prices on ammunition. We sell at a fraction
of any market value.
2) Review HELP SHIP WEAPONS for the costs. Consider nondecaying your
weapons with SHIP REQUISITION NONDECAY weaponname.
3) SHIP AMMO anywhere on the ship will allow you to store, remove, or
check what you have. SHIP WEAPON STATUS anywhere on the ship will
confirm location and status of all weapons.

FIGUREHEAD

1) Review CLHELP SEASPELLS for instructions

NAMING AND FLAGGING

1) Disembark, with a bottle of strong drink on hand
2) SHIP NAME to name your ship
3) Board, and use SHIP SET ALIAS to create a short name for your ship
4) Obtain a flag with a recognisable coat of arms. Hashan sells Hashani
flags, with our city arms over purple or violet, which we recommend all
allied ships fly at all times
5) SHIP HOIST FLAG, from the quarterdeck or bow.
