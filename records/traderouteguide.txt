I. Introduction

Ship trading is one of those things that seems simple at first, but can
be a bit confusing to the uninitiated. Although the official and clan
help scrolls are a great place to start, it sometimes helps to see that
information in a different format. I found that charting it out like
this before I started helped a great deal.

So let's get started!


II. Preparation

It's always good to prepare for your route beforehand. That way, you
have a ready-made list of where you need to go and what cargo you need
to pick up.

Get yourself a pencil and a nice big sheet of parchment. You will want
to spread this sheet out atop your desk - it will help you excel in
creating your tradelists. (Yes, I did say EXCEL).

Look at the clan trade help scrolls (the tradedealchart is very helpful
here) and jot down the following information:

(don't worry, I'll show you how to arrange it in just a bit!)

Starting Harbours
Mid-route Harbours
Ending Harbour
The cargo you will be trading, and the ratios (4:3, 3:2, etc.)

Put this information into down onto your spread-out sheet of parchment
like so. For this example, we're going to use the following trade deal:

"Deliver 5 marble to Tasur'ke Harbour in return for crew morale (ship)."

Harbour | Inbound Cargo |  # In   | # Out   | Outbound Cargo | Ratio |
Shastaan| (nothing)     | XXXXXXX |         |  Sandstone     |  n/a  |
Tasur'ke| Sandstone     |         |         |  Granite       |  4:3  |
Karbaz  | Granite       |         |         |  Marble        |  3:2  |
Tasur'ke| Marble        |         | XXXXXXX |  (nothing)     |  n/a  |

This should be fairly self-explanatory, but I'll go over it right quick:

Harbour - A stop along your trade route.
Inbound Cargo - The TYPE of cargo you are bringing INTO a harbour.
# In - AMOUNT of cargo you are bringing INTO a harbour.
# Out - AMOUNT of cargo you are LEAVING with.
Outbound Cargo - TYPE of cargo you are LEAVING with.
Ratio - The ratio of exchange between two cargo types.


III. Calculations

For our example above, the calculations are fairly straightforward.

1. Put a number into the first "# Out" box. We'll use "12" for this
example.
2. Next, put that same number in the "# In" box on the line just below
it.
3. Take that "# In" number and find the "# Out" number as so:

  # Out = (# In) / (Ratio as a fraction)

For this first one, it would be:
  # Out = 12 / (4/3)
        =  9
		   
There you go - if you bring 12 Sandstone from Shastaan to Tasur'ke, you
will be able to exchange it for 9 Granite. The calculation for the trade
at Karbaz is similar, just change the numbers and ratio:

  # Out =  9 / (3/2)
        =  6

At this point, just take that final "# Out" and stick it in the row for
your final stop - and you're done!

Harbour | Inbound Cargo |  # In   | # Out   | Outbound Cargo | Ratio |
Shastaan| (nothing)     | XXXXXXX |   12    |  Sandstone     |  n/a  |
Tasur'ke| Sandstone     |   12    |    9    |  Granite       |  4:3  |
Karbaz  | Granite       |    9    |    6    |  Marble        |  3:2  |
Tasur'ke| Marble        |    6    | XXXXXXX |  (nothing)     |  n/a  |

So, by using this method, we can see that on this route, if we leave
Shastaan with 12 Sandstone, we can end up back in Tasur'ke with 6 Marble
- enough for our trade! Of course, we will have a few extras left over
at the end, but that's no worry - more for next time!


IV. Conclusion

This technique can be applied to any trade route - just write it out as
shown here, and play with the numbers by starting with your first
outbound cargo and working your way down. Remember to start out with the
correct amount of cargo at the beginning, to get as few leftovers as
possible.

Thanks for reading, and may the trades be in your favour and the winds
at your backs!
