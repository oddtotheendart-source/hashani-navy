clhelp weapons
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 18th of Mayan, 832 AF.

    Weapons Specialisation

Specialist: Hawkins, found near Shastaan's harbour

In addition to the abilities listed below, each rank in Weapons will
also decrease the amount of time it takes to a fire a ship weapon.

Note that no new abilities are gained at Rank IV.

====== Rank I ======

--Status--
    SHIP WEAPON STATUS

Gain an overview of all the ship's weapons, including the details of
their ammunition, damage and months of usefulness remaining.

--Weapons Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, everyone onboard will
aim ship weapons faster. This aura scales with your rank in Weapons.

--Loadweapon--

Allows you to load ammo into ship weapons without incurring a balance
cost.


====== Rank II ======

--Ammo Enhancement--
    LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo> DIPPED

Allows you to enhance certain types of ammunition by coating them with
dragon's tears, acquired from Yabbahar on Zanzibaar.

Effect of dragon's tears:
* starshot: sets the target ship's bow on fire
* chainshot: sets the target ship's sails on fire
* darts: sets the target ship's deck on fire


====== Rank III ======

--Weapon Maintenance--
    SHIP WEAPON MAINTAIN <weapon>
    Comms needed: 1 wood, 1 iron, 1 rope

Allows you to reduce the time to decay on a ship weapon. Eight weapon
maintenance sessions will add one month to the life of the weapon.


====== Rank V ======

--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect increases the pace at which attacked ships leak.
