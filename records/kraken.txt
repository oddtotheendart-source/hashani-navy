Icraa's quick tips on fighting the kraken
- you want to start the fight, firing at TENTACLE and not SEAMONSTER,
since you might find yourself trying to aim for the kraken itself while
one of its tentacles is wrapping itself around your ship

- go around near the edge in a circle, most of the times everyone does
this in a clockwise motion

- try to stay at the max of ballista range, the kraken really hurts up
close (and rip you off the figurehead) but cant even touch you if you
are far enough away

- if you find yourself getting hit by the kraken, your ONLY priority is
to get outside his hitting range, if your hull gets low dont be afraid
to go to the very edge and anchor down for repairs, and keep an eye on
the incoming kraken, as it most likely have not lost sight of you

- having one on your ship hitting with starshots and spidershots can
make it a bit easier should you be so unfortuante to find yourself
inside the reach of the kraken

- finally, every ship that survives the battle also gets a tentacle, so
pace yourself according to your own skill level, try to stay afloat and
good luck!
