Composed by: Minkai on or about the 15th of Mayan, 832 AF.

    Command Specialisation

Specialist: Aitroyo, on Zanzibaar


====== Rank I ======

--Command Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, invoking seaspells uses
less figurehead power. This aura scales with your rank in Command.


====== Rank II ======

--Fleetsense--
    FLEETSENSE

Get detailed information on the ships upon which you have captain perms.
This includes hull and sails damage, strongbox, provisions, current
flag, and the date the ship was last docked.


====== Rank III ======

--Keelhaul--
    KEELHAUL <target>

Does great damage to the target if the ship is moving.


====== Rank IV ======

--Comm Screen--
    SHIP COMMSCREEN CHECK
    SHIP COMMSCREEN RAISE
    SHIP COMMSCREEN LOWER

Removes everyone on board from all WHO lists and prevents the sending
and receiving of tells. However, tells to those on other ships will
still work.


====== Rank V ======

--Remotebuoy--
    SHIP BUOY ACTIVATE <buoy>
    SHIP CARGOBOX TRANSFER <box>
    SHIP CARGOBOX DUMP <box>

Allows you to activate a buoy from a ship that you don't own, so long as
you have captain permissions on the ship that owns the buoy.


--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect allows you to invoke seaspells while not melded with
the figurehead, excluding wavecall. However, they will cost 1.5x the
normal power.

clhelp deckhand
Composed by: Tydas on or about the 18th of Aeguary, 720 AF.

    Deckhand Specialisation

Specialist: Rorthgar, on the Ilyrean Isle

NOTE - Due to the out-of-the-way location of this specialist,
please ask on the Hashani Navy clan for help if you're having
trouble finding a ride up north. Several Maritime aides can
bring a city ship specially docked on the Isle down to the
northern part of the Achaean wilderness, giving you a fast and
easy ride to the specialist.

Rank I
    --Deckhand Aura--

    Allows you to activate this aura. While active, there is a
    small chance of an increased repair amount on a repair
    tick. This aura scales with your rank in Deckhand.


Rank II
    --Salvageops--
        SHIP SALVAGE SCAN
        SHIP SALVAGE <ship id>
        SHIP SALVAGE STOP

    While anchored above a shipwreck, this ability allows you
    to raise the ship from the sea floor, setting it alongside
    your own ship after a period of time. Salvaging a wreck
    requires a ship equipped with a salvage crane. Salvageops
    also comes with the ability to scan for nearby sunken ships
    in order to know where you should anchor to raise it.


Rank III
    --Cooking Good Stores--

    This rank enhances the general Seafaring Cooking ability,
    allowing any food stores you cook to be of good quality
    instead of merely common.

Rank IV
    --Plundering--
        SHIP PLUNDER <full name/short name>
        SHIP PLUNDER

    This gives you the ability to send your ship's
    swashbucklers over to another ship that is grappling or has
    been grappled by your ship. The swashbucklers will attempt
    to relieve the contents of the strongbox and the ship's
    trade cargo and bring them back to your ship. Attacking
    swashbucklers are more likely to suffer losses than those
    defending, and subsequent plunderings will decrease your
    chances at success and increase the danger to the invading
    crew.


Rank V
    --Fire Suppression--

    This passive effect gives a chance for fires on your ship
    to be put out by your crew automatically.

clhelp helm
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 18th of Chronos, 843 AF.

    Helm Specialisation

Specialist: Ambo, on Zaphar Island

The Helm specialisation grants the ability to use manoeuvres.


====== Rank I ======

--Helm Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, ship crew receives a
bonus to experience and morale gain. This aura scales with your rank in
Helm.


====== Rank II ======

--Steadygoing--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE STEADYGOING

Begin manoeuvres that will provide a steadier base for your ship's
weapons.
  - Decreases weapon aim time**
  - Increases ship turn time
  - Increases ship speed
  **If you have both WEAPONS 5 and HARPOONER trait, steadygoing will not
    reduce your weapon aim time further.

--Emergencies--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE EMERGENCIES

Initiate emergency manoeuvres in difficult circumstances.
  - Decreases ship turn time
  - Increases the time your ship stays afloat after the emergency siren
    go off


====== Rank III ======

--Smart Orders--
    SHIP SMARTORDERS

Increase the turn speed of the ship by up to 20% for two minutes. After
the effect of smart orders wears off, smart orders cannot be used on the
same crew for the next 10 minutes.


====== Rank IV ======

--Evasives--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE EVASIVES

Manoeuvre your ship in an erratic fashion.
  - Decreases ship turn time
  - Increases the time it takes for other ships to fire at you

--Jink--
    JINK CLEAR|LARBOARD or LEFT|STARBOARD or RIGHT|SHOW

Allows you to set a jink direction for the ship to avoid ship
collisions.


====== Rank V ======

--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect increases the row speed of the ship by about 1kt.
It also increases the experience and morale gained by denizen crew.

clhelp specialisations
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 13th of Glacian, 869 AF.

  --Specialisation Points (SPPs)--
Specialisation points are gained as you grow more proficient in the
skill of seafaring until you transcend the skill. You get a couple
points immediately after becoming an unquestioned seafarer, but you must
use lessons to gain more by learning more seafaring. At trans seafaring,
you will have 32 SPPs.


  --Spending Specialisation Points--

One question often asked is "how should I spend my specialisation points
(SPPs)?"

To answer that question, you must first understand what purpose each
specialisation (spec) serves on a ship and how specialisations work.

There are five specs that one may train and each one can go up to rank
five. Each rank consumes the same number of SPPs as the rank. So for
example, rank one deckhand consumes one SPP, rank two deckhand consumes
two SPPs, rank three deckhand consumes three SPPs, and so on with a
total of fifteen SPPs to get to rank five.

The specs are as follows: deckhand, weapons, helm, watch, command. Each
one serves a specific set of duties that may be needed aboard a ship.

Deckhands perform basic duties pertaining to maintenance. People who
choose this spec gain a bonus to maintaining the hull and sails of a
ship and can salvage a sunken ship (as long as the rescue ship has a
salvage crane). They can also plunder other ships in a combat situation.
Their aura provides a bonus to the repair speed of a ship. CLHELP
DECKHAND for more.


Those who choose the weapons specialisation deal directly with the
weapons aboard a ship. They are able to dip ammo in dragon tears which
will set other ships on fire and repair weapons. The navy recommends
that all sailors have this specialisation up to RANK 2. Their aura
provides a bonus to the speed of weapon aiming. CLHELP WEAPONS for more.


The Helm specialisation allows for easier navigation over the open
oceans and quicker training of crew. They are masters of tricky
maneuvers that can help the ship turn more quickly or even slip around a
ship on a collision course. Their aura provides a small bonus to row
speed and a bonus to things that increase crew experience, and increase
crew morale, such as cooking. CLHELP HELM for more.


Watchmen are the eyes of the ship. Primarily utilizing the crow's nest,
they watch for other ships and keep the captain informed of any new
developments upon the sea. They can use spyglasses to gain detailed
information about the surroundings and anticipate the movement of other
ships. Their aura provides a speed bonus to climbing the rigging and the
mast. CLHELP WATCH for more.

The command specialisation is all about command. Sailors specialised in
this will be able to use seaspells without invoking the sea spirits, be
able to observe the status of their fleet at once and keelhaul unruly
adventurer crew members. Their aura reduces the cost of seaspells.
CLHELP COMMAND for more.


Once you understand each specialisation, you must ask yourself: "what
purpose do I wish to serve aboard a ship?" Do you want to be a deckhand
and help maintain the ship? Or would you rather take a direct hand in
sinking other ships with the weapon specialisation?

Furthermore, do you plan to sail alone, or in a group? Do you plan to
dedicate yourself to sinking other ships or hunting the monsters of the
seas?

Keep in mind that you have enough SPPs at transcendent seafaring to get
to rank five in any two specialisations, so your duties are not limited
to a single spec. Also keep in mind that as long as you have access to
the specialisation teachers, you can change your specialisations at
will.

clhelp watch
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 16th of Daedalan, 833 AF.

   Watch Specialisation

Specialist: Natalie, found in Shala-Khulia (accessible via ferry from
the Aalen)


====== Rank I ======

--Watch Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, everyone onboard will
climb mast and swing up to the riggings faster. This aura scales with
your rank in Watch.


====== Rank II ======

--Spyglass--
    SPYGLASS LOOK
    SPYGLASS INFO <seamonster>

Requires a spyglass in inventory to use.

Look will display any seamonsters or ships in a small radius around your
ship. Info <seamonster> will show you specific information, such as
health remaining, about a nearby seamonster.


====== Rank III ======

--Anticipate--
    SHIP ANTICIPATE SHOW
    SHIP ANTICIPATE STOP
    SHIP ANTICIPATE START <full or short name of ship in range>

Anticipate the movement of ships in range. Shows you the direction the
ship is heading, the direction the ship is turning to, and if the ship
stops rowing and strikes sails, or starts rowing and sets the sails.


====== Rank IV ======

--Enhanced SHIPWHO and SHIPSCAN--
    SHIPWHO <full or short name of ship in range>
    SHIPSCAN <full name of ship in range>

This ability allows you to see who is present on a different ship.

====== Rank V ======

--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect makes climbing mast and climbing rigging
balanceless.

clhelp weapons
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 18th of Mayan, 832 AF.

    Weapons Specialisation

Specialist: Hawkins, found near Shastaan's harbour

In addition to the abilities listed below, each rank in Weapons will
also decrease the amount of time it takes to a fire a ship weapon.

Note that no new abilities are gained at Rank IV.

====== Rank I ======

--Status--
    SHIP WEAPON STATUS

Gain an overview of all the ship's weapons, including the details of
their ammunition, damage and months of usefulness remaining.

--Weapons Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, everyone onboard will
aim ship weapons faster. This aura scales with your rank in Weapons.

--Loadweapon--

Allows you to load ammo into ship weapons without incurring a balance
cost.


====== Rank II ======

--Ammo Enhancement--
    LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo> DIPPED

Allows you to enhance certain types of ammunition by coating them with
dragon's tears, acquired from Yabbahar on Zanzibaar.

Effect of dragon's tears:
* starshot: sets the target ship's bow on fire
* chainshot: sets the target ship's sails on fire
* darts: sets the target ship's deck on fire


====== Rank III ======

--Weapon Maintenance--
    SHIP WEAPON MAINTAIN <weapon>
    Comms needed: 1 wood, 1 iron, 1 rope

Allows you to reduce the time to decay on a ship weapon. Eight weapon
maintenance sessions will add one month to the life of the weapon.


====== Rank V ======

--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect increases the pace at which attacked ships leak.
