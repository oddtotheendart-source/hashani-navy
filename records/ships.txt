clhelp ships
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 11th of Daedalan, 946 AF.


The Court of Shadows currently owns seven Navy ships that you can be
approved to sail! Please contact the Maritime Minister or one of the
AIDES MARITIME to begin this process if desired.

After using a Navy ship, you are required to ensure that all stores and
strongboxes are restored to an acceptable level and the ship is safely
docked. If you sink, you are going to need to replace the token stores.
You are welcome to ask the Navy for assistance if you do not know how to
do this.
