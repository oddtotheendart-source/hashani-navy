Composed by: Minkai on or about the 18th of Chronos, 843 AF.

    Helm Specialisation

Specialist: Ambo, on Zaphar Island

The Helm specialisation grants the ability to use manoeuvres.


====== Rank I ======

--Helm Aura--

Allows you to activate this aura. While active, ship crew receives a
bonus to experience and morale gain. This aura scales with your rank in
Helm.


====== Rank II ======

--Steadygoing--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE STEADYGOING

Begin manoeuvres that will provide a steadier base for your ship's
weapons.
  - Decreases weapon aim time**
  - Increases ship turn time
  - Increases ship speed
  **If you have both WEAPONS 5 and HARPOONER trait, steadygoing will not
    reduce your weapon aim time further.

--Emergencies--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE EMERGENCIES

Initiate emergency manoeuvres in difficult circumstances.
  - Decreases ship turn time
  - Increases the time your ship stays afloat after the emergency siren
    go off


====== Rank III ======

--Smart Orders--
    SHIP SMARTORDERS

Increase the turn speed of the ship by up to 20% for two minutes. After
the effect of smart orders wears off, smart orders cannot be used on the
same crew for the next 10 minutes.


====== Rank IV ======

--Evasives--
    SHIP MANOEUVRE EVASIVES

Manoeuvre your ship in an erratic fashion.
  - Decreases ship turn time
  - Increases the time it takes for other ships to fire at you

--Jink--
    JINK CLEAR|LARBOARD or LEFT|STARBOARD or RIGHT|SHOW

Allows you to set a jink direction for the ship to avoid ship
collisions.


====== Rank V ======

--Max Aura Bonus--

This passive effect increases the row speed of the ship by about 1kt.
It also increases the experience and morale gained by denizen crew.
