clhelp deepseatreasure
Composed by: Rhydian on or about the 8th of Glacian, 883 AF.

If you like to deepsea dive, this is a list of treasures that can be
found deep in the oceans. They are listed in order of rarity, from
common junk to valued treasures.

a silver seashell hand mirror
a tarnished silver bell
a corroded key
a rune-inked amphora
a small chest decorated with mermaids
a miniscule icon
a water-worn idol
A long, humanoid bone
a battered old music box
a jawless humanoid skull
a complex key
an onyx pendant
a rusty, oyster-encrusted axe
rusty iron shackles
a barnacled sextant
a dodecahedral box
an antique mahogany music box
a curved steel cutlass with a mermaid hilt
a chipped glass goblet
a barnacle-encrusted narwhal horn
a clear glass jar
a bone dagger etched with scrimshaw
a clawed gauntlet
a thin ring
a rotting unicorn figurehead

You can get these by using a shovel to dig in each location, by killing
things, and opening chests.

Coral chests - smaller amounts of gold, opened by turn/push/pull/twist
chest over and over until unlocked.

Steel strongbox chests - open via 'turn chest handle right' once and
waiting a long period of time.

Note: Some chests may require a key fallen from a kill or dug up.

Note: If you don't own the artefact shovel, it is advisable to take more
than one with you, as they can break down there, leaving you
shovel-less.
