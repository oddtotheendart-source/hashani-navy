Modern Ship Buying For The Discerning Captain
By Kuriel Al'Jafri

THE SHORT VERSION

Usually the most cost-effective way to buy a new ship is to collect the
maximum number of shiplines and shipcloth allowed for your desired ship
type in HELP SHIPTYPES. As long as those commodities are outside of your
rift when you COMMISSION, you will receive a discount on the total cost
and you will be quoted a price about 70 percent of the total commission
cost.

The aforementioned applies in the era in which I am writing, in which
rope and cloth are plentiful, iron and steel are only moderately
expensive, and wood prices are ridiculously high.

AN EXAMPLE

Let us say you wish to purchase a brand new strider. Without any
commodities, it will cost you 5 million gold. With all of the suggested
commodities on HELP SHIPTYPES, it will cost you 1,250,000 gold. Because
of many considerations explained below, the bottom line is that you'll
want to collect 300 shiplines and 300 shipiron (the maximum allowed in
HELP SHIPTYPES), remove those 600 commodities from your rift, WALKTO
MAELSTROM and COMMISSION SEASTRIDER. Instead of the full price of 5
million gold, he will quote you 3,950,000 gold. Unless you have access
to free wood, iron, and steel, this is currently your best discount.

THE BRIEF DETAILS

Unless comm prices drastically change, you'll want to exclusively
collect shiplines and shipcloth and pay for the rest of the ship out of
pocket. Here's what you'll need:

Cutter: 1.5K Iron, 7.5K Cloth, 7.5K Rope

Stride: 3K Iron, 15K Cloth, 15K Rope

Galley: 6K Iron, 30K Cloth, 30K Rope

Bring those comms to any shipfitter, and ask them to make the maximum
amount of shipcloth and shiplines available to the ship of your choice
in HELP SHIPTYPES. Outrift those, walk to your ship vendor, commission
the vessel, and see the discount.

THE FULL DETAILS

The full cost of each ship type, without any shipcomms, is:

Cutter: 2.5 million gold
Strider: 5 million gold
Galley: 10 million gold

But when commissioning a ship, each shipcomm that you are holding (not
in your rift) is assigned a gold value that is deducted from the total
cost of the ship, as long as the number of shipcomms that you're using
does not exceed the figures in HELP SHIPTYPES. In the ship market, each
shipcomm is valued at:

Shipwood: 3000 gold
Shipiron: 2250 gold
Shipcloth: 1750 gold
Shiplines: 1750 gold
Pitch: 3000 gold

That is not remotely its market value, because a shipcomm is a composite
of normal commodities prepared by a shipfitter. Here's the conversion
rate:

Shipwood: 50 wood and 10 iron
Shipiron: 50 iron and 10 steel
Shipcloth: 50 cloth
Shiplines: 50 rope and 10 iron
Pitch: 50 wood and 10 animal fat

As you may already have noticed, the ship market seriously undervalues
most commodities. Although 1 shipwood deducts 3000 gold from your ship
purchase, in the current market it would cost something like 10,000 gold
to obtain 1 shipwood. However, it also overvalues some commodities. 1
shipcloth deducts 1750 gold from your ship purchase, but at current
market prices it would only cost 900 to purchase 1 shipcloth. Therefore,
it pays to know the comms market, and to have a general idea of where
shipcomms save money and where they cost it.

Below is the total breakdown of comm costs for each ship type, converted
to conventional comms so that you can easily check prices via CM LIST.
Since we do conclude that, in most cases, you're best off buying just
shipcloth/shiplines and paying for the rest in gold, the conversion rate
for just that method is also included below.

CUTTER
Max Shipcomms: 100 pitch, 200 shipwood, 200 shipiron, 150 shiplines, 150
shipcloth
Total Comms Cost: 15K Wood, 13.5K Iron, 2K Steel, 7.5K Cloth, 7.5K Rope,
1K Fat
Total Shipcloth/Shiplines Alone: 1.5K Iron, 7.5K Cloth, 7.5K Rope

STRIDER
Max Shipcomms: 200 pitch, 400 shipwood, 400 shipiron, 300 shiplines, and
300 shipcloth.
Total Comms Cost: 30K Wood, 27K Iron, 4K Steel, 15K Cloth, 15K Rope, 2K
Fat
Total Shipcloth/Shiplines Alone: 3K Iron, 15K Cloth, 15K Rope

GALLEY
Max Shipcomms: 400 pitch, 800 shipwood, 800 shipiron, 600 shiplines, 600
shipcloth
Total Comms Cost: 60K Wood, 54K Iron, 8K Steel, 30K Cloth, 30K Rope, 4K
Fat
Total Shipcloth/Shiplines Alone: 6K Iron, 30K Cloth, 30K Rope

Unfortunately, the formula for figuring out the value that the ship
market places on any one commodity requires a few simultaneous
equations, none of which are pretty. For the mathematically inclined,
they are:

Shipwood: 300 gp= 5*wood + iron
Shipiron: 225 gp= 5*iron + steel
Shipcloth: 175gp= 5*cloth
Shiplines: 175gp= 5*rope + iron
Pitch: 300gp= 5*wood + fat

It would make little sense to solve any of these equations in a scroll,
because the market value of each commodity is constantly in flux. But
given the current market value of any commodity, it should be
easy enough to use the above equations to figure out if you happen to
have found a good deal, assuming you're buying all of the comms needed
for that particular shipcomm. The mathematically inclined will note that
there's no real way of figuring out the objective value of any of those
comms except for cloth (35gp). The even more mathematically inclined may
figure out a way and make me look pretty silly for saying that.

Special prize to anyone who can figure out, based on all of this, the
ship market value of each commodity. Until then, I leave you with the
unsolved math and wish you fair winds and following seas.
