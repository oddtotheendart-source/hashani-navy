clhelp needammo
Composed by: Nezaya on or about the 11th of Ero, 946 AF.

If you are going to be shooting things/people at sea, you are going to
need ammo!

Currently, we are allowing citizens to buy ammo from the Navy. If this
status should change, this scroll will be altered to reflect it.

You are required to leave a minimum of 1000 ammo of any time on the Navy
ships. If it is below this, please alert the Admiral and do not take any
of that type from that ship.

We have presently set the price for ammunition at 100 gold per unit.
This is significantly cheaper than broadly available market price.

But wait! What if you want to actually donate extra ammo you have
gathering dust to the Navy so that Hashani can buy it and our Navy can
be strong and full of things to shoot at people? Please just go ahead
and store it on any Navy ship and message the Admiral so they can say
thank you.


Useful information on ship ammo:

DEPOSIT ## GOLD HN <reason>

SHIP AMMO STATUS

SHIP AMMO REMOVE ## <ammo type>

SHIP AMMO STORE <ammo type> (no number!)

Please do not hesitate to ask the Admiral or any Maritime Aides any
questions you have regarding this. Happy sailing! Set them all on fire.
