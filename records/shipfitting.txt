clhelp shipfitting
Composed by: Rhydian on or about the 23rd of Ero, 886 AF.

Shipfitters (TRADEWHO SHIPFITTING) can dye sails and paint, stain, or
trim ship hulls. These modifications are visible when you SHIP LOOK from
aboard the ship, SHIP LOOK <dir> at an adjacent ship, or SHIP LOOK
<alias> while standing at the harbour. Dyed sails are visible on
shipwarning.

Ships that have already been prettified using an artefact pail must be
restored before they can be further modified. A shipfitter can restore
the ship using ship commodities.

To decorate a ship, you will need shipfitting supplies that are made
from regular comms. A shipfitter can prepare the following:
Shipwood:  50 wood and 10 iron
Shipiron:  50 iron and 10 steel
Shipcloth: 50 cloth
Shiplines: 50 rope and 10 iron
Pitch:     50 wood and 10 animal fat

To dye the sails of a ship, you will require a mixture of red, blue and
yellow pigments and these base costs:
Windcutter: 15 shipcloth. (750 regular cloth)
Seastrider: 30 shipcloth. (1500 regular cloth)
War Galley: 60 shipcloth. (3000 regular cloth)

To paint/stain a ship, you will require a mixture of red, blue and
yellow pigments and these base costs:
Windcutter: 5 shipwood. (250 wood 50 iron)
Seastrider: 15 shipwood. (750 wood 150 iron)
War Galley: 30 shipwood. (1500 wood 300 iron)

Base costs for hull trims:

Seastrider: 10 shipwood and 10 shipiron. (500 wood 600 iron 100 steel)
War Galley: 20 shipwood and 20 shipiron. (1000 wood 1200 iron 200 steel)
