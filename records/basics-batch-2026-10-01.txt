clhelp whatnow
Composed by: Elyra on or about the 20th of Daedalan, 833 AF.

Hail, Sailor!

You've joined the Navy and you want to help out, but you're not sure
how? Great, you're in the right place.

Firstly, you don't need skill in seafaring to be useful, though it will
help. Getting your marque (HELP MARQUE) even if you can't put lessons in
seafaring yet will also help you gain your sea legs.

Everyone in the Navy should, irrelevant of skill in seafaring, have this
equipment on hand at all times, and know how to use it:
An empty bucket (HELP BUCKETS, bought from Rurin's shops in the Bazaar)
A piece of rope (Commodity - bought from the City commodity shop in the
Bazaar, or the CM)
-With the rope and bucket in your inventory (stash this in your rift
when you leave realm or it will drop out) you can FILL BUCKET WITH WATER
from a ship's deck.
-Then you can DOUSE ROOM to put out a fire once you've located it.

*A magi can also FLOOD the room to put out fires.

A ship's axe (Crafted by a Shipfitter for 15 wood and 15 iron, TRADEWHO
SHIPFITTING)
-When an enemy ship fires a hook shot at your ship, this will tether
your ships together and stop you from escaping, find the hook and CHOP
HOOK or CHOP TETHER to get rid of it. This is a CHANNELED action, that
means it will take time to complete and you can interrupt it if you try
to do anything before it's finished.

Once you have these things and know how to use them reliably and
quickly, congrats, you can ask to be moved to the role of 'Deckhand' in
the Navy. You are now a useful part of the crew!

If you do have some Seafaring Promotion Points (SPP), then you're even
more useful! The first, most important skill you can pick up is weapons
II. This only takes 3 SPP. It will allow you to load a weapon without
using balance and dip your ammunition to set your target on fire. Make
sure your reflexes allow you to quickly do the following:

-Load each of the three ship weapons with their various types of
ammunition, both dipped and undipped. (HELP SHIP WEAPONS) and know what
each does.
-Fire these weapons at ships (your captain will lock your target, you
just need to FIRE WEAPON once your ammo is ready. For this you use
dipped ammunition if a vat is available and it makes sense for that
ammunition.
-Fire these weapons at seamonsters (You need to FIRE BALLISTA AT
SEAMONSTER, for example), you will always use undipped ammo for
seamonsters, dipping it is just wasting your vats.
-Know how to CLEAR weapons (CLEAR BALLISTA, CLEAR THROWER, CLEAR ONAGER)
when they are hit with Spidershot.
-Know how to access the sails and rigging and CLEAR them too, and DOUSE
SAILS if they're on fire.
-Know how to AURA ACTIVATE and AURA SWITCH and what Auras you have
available at what levels.

NOTE: CLEARING and FIRING are both channeled actions and can be
interrupted by your curing or actions.

For further information about how to progress in the Navy, read CLHELP
PROGRESSION.

clhelp progression
Composed by: Jamieo on or about the 23rd of Valnuary, 961 AF.

These are the five roles of the Hashani Navy.

CREWMEN
- A rank for those who do not yet feel confident on the sea, or are not
interested in captaining Hashani naval ships.
- For very new sailors, this role can captain naval ships under the
observation of a Teacher.
- This rank will permit you to crew naval ships and view the help
scrolls.
- You will become a Crewman upon induction.


CAPTAINS
- A rank for those who know the basics of sailing and the
responsibilities that come with sailing a city ship.
- This rank will permit you to captain naval ships following the
guidelines in CHELP CITYSHIP. Failure to follow these guidelines will
result in the rank being stripped.
- To become a Captain, review CHELP CITYSHIP and speak to a Teacher.


MERCHANT MARINES
- A rank for those who know the basics of sailing and the
responsibilities that come with sailing a city ship, but who DO NOT want
to engage in ship combat.
- Merchant Marines have a right to take ships out for voyages, trading,
and exploration. Merchant Marines are not expected to crew combat ships,
but can if they like.
- This rank will permit you to captain naval ships following the
guidelines in CHELP CITYSHIP. Disobeying the guidelines will result in
the rank being stripped.
- To become a Merchant Marine, speak with the Admiral or an Aide.


TEACHERS
- A rank for those who have experience sailing and are willing to teach
others or answer questions.
- This rank will allow you to share captain perms to Crewmen while under
observation, and progress Crewmen to Captains after a short, verbal
examination.
- To become a Teacher, speak to the Admiral.


AIDES
- A rank for those who are willing to assist in the maintenance of the
Hashani Navy fleet and/or its help scrolls.
- This rank allows access to the funds for refilling strongboxes, and
permits editing help scrolls within the clan.
- To become an Aide, speak to the Admiral.

    Seafaring Skill Information

The following are a series of charts demonstrating how skill progression
works in Seafaring. The first chart shows how many lessons in total are
needed for each ability. In the upper right is a chart showing how many
SPP's are gained at each rank in Seafaring. The last five charts show
what you gain in the specialisation, and the rank you gain it it. Some
specialisations don't have advantages at certain ranks, and some have
multiple abilities per rank.

To see details on each specialisation's ability, and the location of the
teacher for that specialisation, please check CLHELP <specialisation>.

Also remember that the SPP cost for a rank in a specialisation increases
by one for each rank you've already obtained. A maximum of fifteen SPP's
are required to master a specialisation.
