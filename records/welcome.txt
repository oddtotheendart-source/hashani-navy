clhelp welcome
Composed by: Minkai on or about the 1st of Lupar, 950 AF.

Welcome to the world of seafaring and the Hashani Navy! We are glad that
you have decided to join our ranks, and look forward to sailing the seas
with you. Feel free to peruse the following scrolls at your leisure, and
as always, we are here to answer questions.


CLHELP WHATNOW
- This scroll talks you through the very basics of Seafaring.
Recommended for our newest members who are still learning to crew.

CLHELP PROGRESSION
- This scroll lays out the roles of the Navy, and the requirements for
each one.

CHELP CITYSHIP
- This scroll details the city policy regarding the use of the Hashan
Navy Fleet. Essential reading for those who want to captain Navy ships,
as misuse of the ships will result in permissions being stripped.

CHELP NAVALPOLICY
- This scroll details the city policy regarding foreigners upon the sea.

- This is a list of all public Seafaring-related scrolls.
