clhelp shipequipment
Composed by: Rhydian on or about the 9th of Glacian, 883 AF.

Shipfitters (TRADEWHO SHIPFITTING) can create many helpful items. The
list below includes summaries of item functions and the commodity cost.
See HELP SHIP EQUIPMENT for full details.

Commodities are available through the commodities market in Hashan and
Delos. Anyone who employs foragers can acquire cloth and rope (see HELP
FORAGING). Miners refine iron and steel. Gatherers can obtain animal fat
from butchering.

Shipcloth:   50x cloth
Shipiron:    50x iron, 10x steel
Shipwood:    50x wood, 10x iron
Shiplines:   50x rope, 10x iron
Pitch:       50x wood, 10x animal fat


==Bait Tank==
40 shipiron   2000 iron, 400 steel
5 pitch       250 wood, 50 animal fat
Bait within the tank decays at a slower rate than usual. Can be placed
in any ship location.

==Beacon of Iocolas==
20 shipiron   1000 iron, 200 steel
50 goldbar
50 stone
Provides more detail in SHIP LIST. Placed on the bow.

==Captain's Trunk==
5 shipwood    250 wood, 50 iron
5 shipiron    250 iron, 50 steel
Placed in the cabin, the trunk prevents swashbucklers from patrolling
within. It is not a usable trunk for holding items.

==Cartographer's Desk ==
Standard:
5 shipwood    250 wood, 50 iron
5 shipiron    250 iron, 50 steel

Large:
15 shipwood   750 wood, 150 iron
15 shipiron   750 iron, 150 steel
Holds ship maps, which can be purchased blank from Maelstrom and scribed
by seafarers while on a ship in port. Placed in the cabin. The ship
captain can get bearings (AB SEAFARING BEARINGS) to mapped harbours.

==Diving Bell==
20 shipwood    1000 wood, 200 iron
10 shiplines   500 rope, 100 iron
10 pitch       500 wood, 100 animal fat
From an anchored ship, a sailor can SHIP ENTER BELL, then SHIP LOWER
BELL to explore the undersea. Once in the bell, SHIP RAISE BELL and SHIP
LEAVE BELL. Placed on the quarterdeck.

==Leadline==
10 leather
40 rope
Used to take soundings to determine if an area is suitable for diving
and fishing. Held in one's inventory.

==Mariner's Sextant==
15 shipiron    750 iron, 150 steel
At noon, SHIP TAKE SIGHTING will provide a rough latitude position. It
can be placed anywhere except the cabin.

==Salvage Crane==
15 shipwood    750 wood, 150 iron
15 shipiron    750 iron, 150 steel
10 shiplines   500 rope, 100 iron
Perform basic salvage operations to recover sunken ships.

==Multipurpose Crane==
20 shipwood    1000 wood, 200 iron
25 shipiron    750 iron, 150 steel
15 shiplines   750 rope, 150 iron
The multipurpose crane and dredge allows you to salvage ships as well as
sunken cargo. SHIP DREDGE gives more detail.

==Ship Axe==
15 wood
15 iron
Speeds up the process of chopping tethers for grappling lines. Held in
inventory.

==Cargo Buoy==
3 shipcloth    150 cloth
3 shiplines    150 rope, 30 iron
Provides protection for cargo when a ship is sunk, usable only one time.
A person with captain permissions on the sunken ship must collect the
cargobox. When two months remain on the buoy, anyone can salvage it. It
can be transferred back to the original ship while both ships are at the
same harbour.

==Cargo Float==
3 shipwood     150 wood, 30 iron
3 shiplines    150 rope, 30 iron
Increases cargo capacity by 25 and lasts thirty months. SHIP INFO
displays time remaining.

==Loudhailer==
10 wood
5 cloth
The loudhailer allows you to HAIL <ship> <message>! <ship> must be one
word, it will work with ship target, ship IDs, partial ship names, and
ship aliases. Everyone on your current ship and the target ship will
hear your hails.

==Bucket==
Allows a mariner to DOUSE a ROOM where fire has been broken
out, even in rigging.
(Purchased in a city)

==Rope==
Required to drop a bucket over the rail to fill it with water.
(Purchased in a city)

==Spyglass==
Allows a mariner to gain information about nearby ports,
vessels, and creatures within certain distances.
(Purchased from Captain Maelstrom)

==Vat==
Used to enhance ammo, giving them a chance to create fires upon
hitting a target.
(Purchased in Zanzibaar)
                                              
==Token==
Allows a mariner to recall from a ship back to Tasur'ke.
(Initially purchased from Captain Maelstrom, can be etched by a
Seafarer)
