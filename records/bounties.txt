Hashan Navy Bounty Board

To claim a bounty, report your successful mission to the Admiral,
Ministry of Defense, or a Regent.
Only one bounty can be claimed at a time.
The board is subject to change at any time.

Eligibility
Be a Petty Officer or Battle Officer.
Complete the bounty as the Captain of an HN vessel.
Sailors of a lower rank can crew for an Officer and use the battle
experience towards promotion.


Permanent Bounties

BOUNTY: Sink a city enemy
REWARD: 30,000 gold
STATUS: OPEN. REPEATABLE
*This can be claimed multiple times at once with a cap of 200k gold per
year*
