                      Recommended     Maximum Useful
                      -----------     --------------
For a War Galley           6                11

Swashbucklers wander around your ship to protect your shipmates (and
you!) in the event of an attack on deck. They are not able to be
assigned a station either and CAN get trapped behind closed doors.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Personnel
Though not necessarily the only option you have, the following are the
crew numbers recommended in general for a successful ship. The numbers
are presented in a format of Crewmates/Shipmates/Swashbucklers.

For a Windcutter: 24/15/3  (72000g to hire)
For a Seastrider: 60/40/5 (225000g to hire)
For a War Galley: 70/50/6 (275000g to hire)

2. New Crew
While training and moralising your crew, it is recommended that you pay
them generous wages and activate Helm aura at the maximum level to keep
their spirits up and help them get the most out of every journey. The
fastest way to boost morale is by cooking stores with Helm aura
maximised. The fastest way to gain experience is by killing seamonsters,
which is also good for morale.

3. Crew Training & Upkeep
Crewmates and shipmates will gradually gain experience from performing
their rowing and repairing duties. Swashbucklers only gain experience
from seamonster kills, crew experience trades, and plundering or being
plundered. Morale needs to be maintained by keeping the crew fed and
paid.
Empty stores or strongboxes will harm morale.

4. Base Wages - The End Goal
Once a ship's crew is fully moralised, their wages can be reduced
gradually, but you will need to compensate for decreased morale each
time you lower them. The minimum pay levels or "base wages" are 4 bits
for crewmates, 6 bits for shipmates, and 2 gold, 4 bits for
swashbucklers. If you top up morale to maximum after dropping the wages
to these levels, it will not decrease anymore due to low wages.
